# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Customers use the portal to submit monitoring requests and follow their orders, mission progress, and next steps.

## Product Purpose

OnDemand Monitor lets customers request drone-based site monitoring and follow quotation, payment, order, mission, and result progress from one portal.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing customer portal supports monitoring requests, order status and details, mission history, a results library, quotations, and invoices. The overview redesign must keep its current routes and data behavior; it must not imply a mission state or customer action that the available order and mission records do not support.

## Brand Commitments

For the customer portal redesign, keep the existing light blue surface, blue primary accent, OnDemand Monitor identity, and Vietnamese/English support.

## Evidence on Hand

The customer overview, order, mission history, and result library screens are present in the frontend. The overview reads customer orders and mission history from their existing APIs.

## Product Principles

- Put order status, mission progress, and the customer's next step first.
- Keep counts and statuses faithful to the records the customer can inspect.
