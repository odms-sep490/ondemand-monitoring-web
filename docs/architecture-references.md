## Key Architectural Characteristics Captured
- **Concept**: "Forested Mountain / Green Mountain" — undulating, staggered floor plates rising from low terraced saddles up to three prominent peaks.
- **Spatial Organization**: Connected multistory academic wings enclosing a large, lush central green courtyard open at the main south entrance gateway.
- **Facade Language**:
  - White (`#E8E8E3`) and light-gray (`#B8BEC4`) architectural concrete slabs, columns, and vertical sun-shading fins.
  - Deeply recessed dark-framed classroom windows (`#5C859C` glass / `#39444D` mullions) paired with alternating protruding concrete planter boxes.
  - Restrained FPT orange (`#F58220`) accents at the main entrance canopy fascia, selected vertical accent fins, and entrance signage monument.
- **Landscape & Rooftops**:
  - Stepped rooftop terrace gardens on every setback level with grass beds, walkways, parapets, and rooftop trees.
  - Central courtyard park with pedestrian walkways, decorative reflection/lotus pond, tiered outdoor seating, benches, flower beds, and tropical/palm trees.
