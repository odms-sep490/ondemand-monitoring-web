# OnDemand Monitor — Generative Art Direction

## 1. Visual Identity
- **Theme**: Premium Cinematic Aerial Technology (consistent with deep aviation navy `#060d19`, cool cobalt `#1565e8`, crisp cyan `#38bdf8`, and clean editorial white `#ffffff`).
- **Photography Rule**: Every service visual shares the same commercial aviation camera language — elevated 3/4 aerial angle, natural late-afternoon / morning-mist lighting, realistic enterprise quadcopter scale, crisp optical clarity, zero embedded text, zero watermarks, zero fabricated HUD overlays or fake logos.
- **Mascot Consistency**: Preserves the approved `/assets/ai/ondemand-ai-mascot.webp` without redesigning it.

## 2. Prompt Engineering Structure
Every generated asset was authored with explicit `SUBJECT`, `COMPOSITION`, `STYLE`, `LIGHTING & COLOR`, and `EXCLUSIONS` directives, with dedicated negative space in `final-cta-drone.png` (left 60% calm deep navy) so white HTML headlines and CTAs maintain WCAG AA+ contrast.

## 3. Turning Static Images into Immersive Visuals
- **Service Explorer**: `AnimatePresence` cross-fade + subtle Ken Burns depth settle (`scale: 1.04 → 1.0`) on active tab switch, paused under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- **Final CTA Band**: Full-bleed twilight drone backdrop behind a directional deep-navy scrim (`linear-gradient(95deg, rgba(6,13,25,0.92) 0%, rgba(6,13,25,0.68) 55%, rgba(6,13,25,0.28) 100%)`) that visually echoes the Hero.
